## The short answer

**Own one audience. Sell one product. Get one result. Repeat.**

- **Audience to own first:** Delaware dog owners who take their dogs places.
- **First product:** Founding Dogs Partner, 90 days, **$500–$750**, one sponsor.
- **First five calls:** Concord Pet · Riverfront Pets · Dog Dayz · Camp Bow Wow
  Delaware North · Fur Baby Pet Resort
- **Next five:** Canalside Inn · Old Inlet Bait & Tackle · Atlantic Cycles ·
  Dewey Beach Watersports · Lefty's Alley & Eats
- **First direct product:** *Delaware With a Dog* digital guide, ~$9.

Why dogs first: beach rules change by town and season, park and dog-park rules
differ, pet fees vary, breweries have inside/outside restrictions, lodging
policies differ — all of it recurring, high-intent, and matched to advertisers
(pet retail, daycare, boarding, grooming, trainers, pet-friendly hotels, vets).

## Two kinds of customer, kept separate

**Audience customers** pay with attention, repeat visits, signups, purchases,
corrections. **Revenue customers** pay to reach them.

> Optimize for advertisers before the site is indispensable to readers, and it
> becomes a weak advertising site.

## Acquisition

1. **Search is the engine.** Build pages as *decision + location + current
   answer*, not inspiration listicles.
2. **Answer in the snippet.** First paragraph stands alone:
   > Yes. Dogs are allowed on Rehoboth Beach from Oct. 1 through April 30. From
   > May 1 through Sept. 30, dogs are not allowed on the beach or boardwalk.
   > Last checked against the city rule Aug. 8, 2026.
3. **Intent pages** (revive `/good-for/` as structured, generated pages): Open
   today · Open Sunday · Free · Restrooms confirmed · Playground + restrooms ·
   Dog can come · Indoors · Shade · Under an hour · Toddler · Hot day · Before
   9am · Dog-friendly drinks · No entrance fee.
4. **Physical distribution.** QR cards in pet stores, hotels, breweries, bike
   rentals, visitor centers, coffee shops, bookstores, bait shops, campgrounds.
   Trackable URLs: `delawarefieldguide.com/go/concordpet`.
5. **Partnerships beat social posting.** A pet store handing the card to a dog
   customer beats another Instagram post.
6. **Email is the repeat channel** — *Checked This Week*, Friday morning.
7. **Social is a distribution layer, not the business.** Post single useful facts,
   link to the permanent checked answer.
8. **Local communities** — answer as a person; the answer must be valuable
   without the click.
9. **Earned media** — pitch a fact, not the website. *"I checked all five beach
   towns and found three different summer dog-rule systems."*

## The product: Founding Field Guide Partner (Dogs), 90 days, $500–750

**Includes:** sponsor unit on Dogs hub + selected dog-rule pages · logo · ≤20-word
message · one CTA · tracked outbound URL · listing on the independence page ·
newsletter placement when live · end-of-term report. Optional tracked offer (free
evaluation, first day free, 10% off, booking code).

**Never includes:** editorial ranking, "verified" status, favorable wording, fact
removal, guaranteed traffic, inclusion in factual results.

**Exclusivity:** by category (one pet retail, one lodging) — not blanket.

### Pricing ladder
- **$500 / 90 days** — founding test, small traffic, no case study yet
- **$750 / 90 days** — vertical has real search traffic and numbers to show
- **$1,000 / 90 days** — high-value booking categories, once conversions exist

Benchmark that local sponsorship is already a familiar budget line: Delaware Small
Business Chamber publishes event sponsorships ~$200–300, 6-month website sponsor
$600, 12-month $975, packages to $1,000–2,500 (dsbchamber.com/sponsors).

**Do not price by CPM yet.** Sell a fixed campaign; report audience and clicks.

## Sequence

**Week 0** — publish independence policy · build one sample sponsor unit (use a
fictional "Example Pet Co.") · track outbound clicks · one-page media sheet ·
prospect spreadsheet.

**Week 1** — contact five (Concord, Riverfront, Dog Dayz, Camp Bow Wow, Fur Baby).
Goal is conversations and objections, not five closes.

**Week 2** — contact five adjacent (Canalside, Old Inlet, Atlantic Cycles, Dewey
Watersports, Lefty's). Learn which category is the easiest buyer.

**Week 3** — close one. If $750 stalls, offer $500 founding. **Do not give it away
free** — a free sponsor teaches nothing about willingness to pay.

**Week 4** — measure and report. Ask the sponsor: did anyone mention the Field Guide?

**Month 2** — launch *Delaware With a Dog*, ~$9 digital.
**Month 3** — second vertical, chosen from actual traffic.
**Month 4** — *Checked This Week* newsletter, one sponsor.
**Month 5** — tourism organizations, with evidence.
**Month 6** — community banks / credit unions.

### The literal first 14 days
1 publish independence page · 2 build sponsor component · 3 add click tracking ·
4 pull analytics · 5 media sheet · 6 prospect sheet · 7 write 5 personalized notes ·
8 email Concord + Riverfront · 9 email Dog Dayz + Camp Bow Wow · 10 email Fur Baby ·
11 call one or two non-responders · 12 record objections, revise · 13 private
mockup (label as concept, not endorsement) · 14 follow up.

> Do not spend those two weeks building a complex ad server.

### Opening lines
- **Concord Pet** — "You have the unusual advantage of being both highly local to Delaware and large enough to serve dog owners across the state."
- **Riverfront Pets** — "A large portion of the northern Delaware dog content is used by the same Wilmington-area dog owners you serve."
- **Dog Dayz** — "Your local/daycare focus makes more sense beside the New Castle County dog pages than a generic national pet ad would."
- **Camp Bow Wow** — "Your existing 'first day free' makes this easy to test because we can track whether readers convert."
- **Fur Baby** — "One of the few that bridges both local dog owners and people bringing dogs to the beaches."
- **Canalside Inn** — "The Field Guide reaches dog owners while they're deciding whether a Rehoboth trip is workable."
- **Old Inlet** — "Our surf-fishing audience is checking the exact rules, crossings and permits that bring anglers into your store."
- **Lefty's** — "The Field Guide's rainy-day problem is your customer acquisition problem."

**Follow-up 1** at 4–5 business days, **follow-up 2** at 7–10 days after, then stop.

### Objections
- *"Traffic too small."* → "Fair. This is a founding test, priced as a fixed 90-day local sponsorship rather than a mature media buy." Reduce once. Never promise future traffic.
- *"Free listing instead?"* → "Editorial listings are already independent of advertising. If you belong in the Field Guide and I can verify it, the listing doesn't require payment."
- *"Call us the best?"* → "No. The sponsor unit can explain why someone should choose you, in your own words."
- *"Remove this negative fact?"* → "If it's inaccurate or out of date I'll fix it after verifying, whether or not you advertise."

### On a call, ask
Which customer do you want more of? · Which area? · What is one customer worth? ·
Call, book, visit, shop? · Do you have a trackable offer? · Which season matters?

**Wholesale is a distribution strategy, not just margin** — a shop that *makes*
money selling the product participates more willingly than one asked to buy ads.

**B2B: "Powered by Delaware Field Guide"** — branded guides for hotels, apartment
complexes, employers, realtors. They buy the wrapper, print run and update period;
the Field Guide keeps editorial control of the facts.

## The flywheel

Search → immediate answer → source/date builds trust → user saves or subscribes →
user reports an update → data improves → ranking improves → more people arrive →
sponsor buys that audience → sponsor funds more checking → product improves.

The unhealthy loop to avoid: need money → add ads → pages get noisier → trust
drops → return use weakens → need more ads.

# Open questions before any of this starts

Recorded here rather than acted on. Flagged in conversation Aug 8.

### 1. There are no audience numbers to pull
Day 4 says "pull current analytics." **There is no analytics on this site** — by
design, per the no-trackers rule. Cloudflare proxy isn't live yet (it's still on
the DNS blocker). Until it is, there is no honest answer to "how much traffic do
you have," and the plan says explicitly: *do not invent audience numbers*.

This blocks the media sheet, so it blocks Week 1. Resolving it is a prerequisite,
not a parallel task. The agreed shape — Cloudflare server-side counting plus
`/go/<sponsor>/` 301s for first-party click counts — satisfies both this plan's
tracking needs and the no-trackers rule, but it has to exist first.

### 2. Outreach is Matt's to send, not mine
This document contains real businesses, real people and real email addresses. I
will draft, research and prepare anything wanted, and I will not send outreach,
sign anything up, or contact any of them. That stays with you.

### 3. The plan's "restore /good-for/" vs. the open task
Intent pages are a good idea and the audit agrees. Task #2 says replace them with
filters. Generated intent pages satisfy both — worth confirming that reading.

### 4. Sponsor placement rules need writing down before the first unit is built
From the audit and your own steer ("as long as it fits the brand and it shows
confirmed data"): no sponsor beside emergency, safety, or children's content;
alcohol only in adult sections; a sponsor must be a real local business whose own
listing meets normal sourcing standards. This belongs in the published
independence policy, which is Day 1.
