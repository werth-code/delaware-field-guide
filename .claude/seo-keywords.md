# Keyword targets — Delaware Field Guide

Audited 4 August 2026. No Ahrefs/Semrush connected, so demand is judged from
SERP composition rather than volume figures. Directional, not precise.

## Where the site was under-targeting itself

| Term | In title/meta/h1 before | After |
|---|---|---|
| dog park (as a phrase) | patchy | 36 of 75 |
| "State Park, Delaware" | 3 of 75 | 17 of 75 |
| dog friendly beaches | 0 | on the dogs index |

The town pages were already right. "Can dogs go on Rehoboth Beach?" is exactly
the query, and it was already the title. That pattern is the model; the rest of
the site should look more like it.

## Who we are actually competing with

For "dogs on Delaware beaches" the first page is a hotel, a realtor's blog, a
restaurant guide and two tourism boards. Not one of them dates a fact or names
a source, and several are still printing Rehoboth's pre-January dates.

That is the entire opening. The play is not to out-write them. It is to be the
only page a person can check.

## Question-shaped, where we already hold the answer

- can dogs go on the beach in delaware
- when are dogs allowed on rehoboth beach
- do you need a permit for surf fishing in delaware
- how much is it to get into a delaware state park
- are dogs allowed on the boardwalk
- which delaware beaches allow dogs in summer

**"Dewey beach dog license" is the sharpest one.** It is a transactional query,
it is nearly uncontested, and Dewey is the only town of the five that requires
one. Most guides do not mention it exists.

## Under-targeted own content

1. **The fee change.** Entry fees roughly doubled in March 2026 and we hold the
   figures with a source. "delaware state park fees" wants the current number.
2. **Restrooms at county parks.** Nobody publishes this and the intent is high.
3. **/changed/** is a genuinely unusual page and gets no keyword targeting.
4. **Surf fishing crossings** dropped their reservation requirement in 2025 and
   plenty of pages still say otherwise.

## Writing rules

- Keep the question h1s. They are the reason the town pages work.
- Say "Delaware" in the title of anything that could be another state's park.
- Never soften a date or a fee to fit a phrase.
